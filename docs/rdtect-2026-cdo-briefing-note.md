## What "Voice Gate" Means

You are not a bottleneck. You are a consistency check.

| You do | You don't do |
|--------|-------------|
| Read drafts for tone + positioning alignment | Write or originate content |
| Flag deviations from the rdtect voice | Block publish (Rick has final say) |
| Ensure RapidAI stays in proof-point position | Edit for grammar or style |
| Respond within 48 hours of draft receipt | Review community comments or X replies |

### The rule:
> Rick writes. CDO reviews for voice. Rick publishes. CMO tracks.

---

## rdtect Voice Reference

From the approved positioning (RDT-249):

- **Audience:** Architects — practicing, aspiring, adjacent (engineering leaders, design technologists)
- **Core thesis:** Architects are natural AI leaders
- **Tone:** Confident, informed, practice-grounded. Not academic. Not hype. Not dev-tool marketing.
- **Hard rule:** RapidAI is a supporting proof point only — never the public lead. rdtect leads. RapidAI proves.

### Voice litmus test (ask these three questions on every draft):

1. **"Does this sound like an architect wrote it?"** — Practice-grounded, systems-thinking, constraint-aware. Not a marketer. Not an engineer. Not an academic.
2. **"Is rdtect leading?"** — If RapidAI appears before rdtect in the reader's mental model, it's wrong.
3. **"Would an architect forward this to another architect?"** — If yes, ship. If they'd hesitate, flag it.

## Process Flow

```
Rick drafts → CMO queues → CDO reviews (48h) → Rick has final say → CMO schedules publish
```

### Step by step:

1. **Rick drafts** a LinkedIn long-form post, blog post, or YouTube script
2. **CMO logs** the draft in the content queue and notifies CDO
3. **CDO reviews** within 48 hours, checking against voice reference above
4. **CDO responds** with one of:
   - ✅ **Clear** — no voice issues, proceed to publish
   - ⚠️ **Flagged** — specific tone/positioning concerns noted (Rick decides)
   - 🔄 **Suggest** — optional refinements offered (Rick decides)
5. **Rick makes final call** — author voice wins over brand polish
6. **CMO schedules** the publish per the cadence calendar
