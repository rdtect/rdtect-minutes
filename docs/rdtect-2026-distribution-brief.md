## 1. Positioning Anchor (Approved — RDT-249)

rdtect is positioned for architects who lead with AI. The frame is authority, not tooling.

- **Audience:** Architects — practicing, aspiring, adjacent (engineering leaders, design technologists)
- **Core thesis:** Architects are natural AI leaders. The practice of architecture — systems thinking, constraint navigation, design under ambiguity — maps directly onto effective AI orchestration.
- **Tone:** Confident, informed, practice-grounded. Not academic. Not hype. Not dev-tool marketing.
- **Hard rule (CEO):** RapidAI is a supporting proof point only — never the public lead. rdtect leads. RapidAI proves.

---

## 2. Channel Sequence

Distribution follows a cascade model: one primary channel drives the next. Each piece earns its place before being amplified.

| Order | Channel | Cadence | Role |
|-------|---------|---------|------|
| **1** | **LinkedIn** | Bi-weekly | Primary publishing surface. Long-form posts establish authority in architecture + AI circles. |
| **2** | **X (Twitter)** | Derived from LinkedIn | LinkedIn posts spawn X threads. Same ideas, tighter format. Reach different circles. |
| **3** | **Blog (rdtect.com/writing)** | Monthly | Canonical home for cornerstone pieces. LinkedIn posts point here for depth. |
| **4** | **YouTube** | Quarterly | Long-form video for framework walkthroughs, talks, deep-dives. Lowest frequency, highest production value. |

### Cascade rule:
> LinkedIn long-form → extract X thread → expand to blog canonical → (optional) video adaptation.  
> Never publish the same piece simultaneously across channels. Let it breathe on one surface before cascading.

## 4. Opening Salvo

CEO specifies two options for the first public move. Pick one and ship it. A published post beats a perfect draft.

### Option A: Aero Window Post
- Personal, immediate, visual
- Shows practice in action — an architect using AI in real work
- Lower stakes, faster to publish
- Good for building initial engagement before the cornerstone

### Option B: Forty-Nine Sessions Post
- Reflective, pattern-surfacing
- "What 49 sessions of AI-augmented architecture work taught me"
- Sets up the cornerstone naturally
- Higher authority, slightly heavier lift

### Recommendation:
If the cornerstone (*Architects Are Natural AI Leaders*) is ready or near-ready, use **Option A (Aero Window)** as the opening salvo — it's lighter, draws attention, and tees up the cornerstone the following week. If the cornerstone needs more time, lead with **Option B (Forty-Nine Sessions)** to establish the practice depth upfront.

**Decision needed from CEO:** Which opening salvo? And is the cornerstone draft ready?

## 9. Voice Gate

**All public content passes through CDO review before publish.**

- CDO is the brand-voice owner per org design
- Rick writes in his voice; CDO ensures consistency with rdtect positioning
- Gate applies to: LinkedIn long-form, blog posts, YouTube scripts
- Gate does not apply to: community comments, X replies, engagement activity

### Process:
1. Rick drafts → CDO reviews for voice/positioning alignment
2. CDO flags tone deviations or positioning drift
3. Rick has final say on publish (author voice wins over brand polish)

## 11. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| **CEO bandwidth** | High | Posts don't ship; cadence breaks | Batch drafting sessions. Opening salvo and cornerstone pre-written before W1. |

| **Channel fragmentation** | Medium | Trying to be everywhere, succeeding nowhere | Strict cascade: LinkedIn → X → Blog → YouTube. Never publish original to secondary channels. |
| **CMO model limitations** | Medium | CMO on deepseek-flash; strategy quality ceiling | CMO handles execution tracking + coordination. CEO + CDO handle creative and strategy. |
